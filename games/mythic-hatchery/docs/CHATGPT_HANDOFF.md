## Key design decisions (already made; don't reopen)
- Species: Dragon, Griffin, Phoenix, Hydra, Unicorn + 6 hidden hybrids (`Species.luau`). Rarities Common→Mythic.
  Elements Fire, Ice, Storm, Nature, Shadow with a beats-cycle (`Elements.luau`).
- Eggs go into incubator slots (timers; 3 slots, +2 with a pass; 2x Incubation pass). Species, rarity and element
  are rolled independently; odds are always shown. Luck boosts every rarity above the egg's lowest.
- Growth Baby → Teen → Adult → Ancient through Feed/Play/Sleep care tasks on cooldowns. Neglect only pauses growth.
- Fusion: two Adult+ creatures → one Fused Egg (parents consumed), rarity upgrade chance, cross-species hybrids.
  Golden/Rainbow crafting is GONE (replaced by fusion + the Neon potion).
- Potions per creature with gems: Ride (150), Fly (300), Neon (200). Adult+ with Ride/Fly can be ridden; fliers race
  in a Sky Arena above the hub (checkpoint rings, server-validated).
- Robux: gems (which buy gem eggs, potions, skips), boosts, passes listed in `Products.luau`. "Pay to get stronger
  faster", never instant wins. Players in regions where paid random items are restricted can't buy gem eggs or luck.
- World: ONE connected land with six regions in a line (Meadow hub, Shadow Grove, Frost Peaks, Storm Coast, Ember
  Volcano, Sky Plateau), gates are archways in natural barriers, no islands. Everything is built from Roblox
  primitives at runtime via data generated by `models/gen_models.py` (Python), no uploaded meshes.
- Kept from Pet Expedition: coin farming on breakables, expeditions, rebirth, daily/playtime/quests, Index, boosts,
  Server Luck, the Creature Ring (walk-in PvP), trading (creatures only, journaled atomic swap), DataStore with
  session locking and receipt dedupe, Studio-only dev panel.
