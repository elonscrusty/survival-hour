# Art direction (from the owner's reference pictures)

The owner picked these ChatGPT concept pictures. All models should match them.

- **Pets (`puppy_variants.jpg`, `pet_ring.jpg`): blocky, voxel-toy style.** Pets are built from cubes and boxes
  (box head bigger than the body, box ears and legs, square paws), with a cute flat face on the front of the head:
  big round black eyes with two white highlights, pink round blush, small black nose, open smiling mouth with a
  pink tongue. Bevel-free, clean colours, chunky proportions (head ~55% of the height).
  - Golden: shiny gold metal all over; eyes, blush, mouth and collar stay normal.
  - Rainbow: horizontal rainbow stripes (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple) across head, body, ears and tail;
    face patch stays cream.
  - Shiny: normal colours, slightly brighter, with gold sparkle particles around it.
- **Eggs (`eggs.jpg`):** each egg sits on a matching themed pedestal: meadow (cream egg, green spots, daisies, grass
  pedestal), grove (purple egg, pink spots, glowing mushrooms), frost (icy blue egg, big snowflake, crystal spikes),
  coral (sand egg, teal waves, shell, coral branches), volcano (dark rock egg with glowing lava cracks, stone base),
  star (deep purple galaxy egg, yellow/pink/blue stars), prism (faceted rainbow crystal on a crystal base).
- **Islands (`islands.jpg`):** blocky floating islands with stepped stone cliffs, waterfalls, rocks in the water,
  stairs carved into cliffs, voxel trees, rope bridges with stone gate pillars and lanterns. Meadow: blocky
  trees and a dirt path; Grove: giant glowing mushrooms; Frost: snowy peaks, ice waterfall; Coral: palms, lagoon,
  coral, waterfall; Volcano: erupting volcano with lava rivers and crystals; Starfall: planets, stars, a swirl portal.
- **Hub (`hub.jpg`):** a stone plaza with a big paw print in the middle; egg on a gold star pedestal with bunting,
  a framed island-map quest board with lanterns, a golden gear machine with a gem on top, a dog statue on a stone
  pedestal with a paw badge, a giant open book on a gold-trimmed stand, a windmill, fences, hay crates, lantern
  posts, flowers and a dock.
- **Pet Ring (`pet_ring.jpg`):** sand floor with stepping-stone spots, red posts with glowing gold balls, white
  ropes, a wooden "PET RING" arch sign at the back, grass tufts and grey rocks around, path to the entrance.
